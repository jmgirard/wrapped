# M020: Stop wraps at block shortcodes

**Status:** done (2026-10-28, PR #22 https://github.com/jmgirard/wrapped/pull/22)

**Goal:** A paragraph that holds only a block shortcode, such as `{{< video >}}`, ends the wrap of the figure before it, as `{{< pagebreak >}}` does now.

**Outcome:** In `wrapped.lua` the shortcode pass (`find_shortcode`) records every shortcode's name by custom id, and `is_block_shortcode` makes `joins_group` stop a group at a paragraph that holds exactly one shortcode outside `JOINING_SHORTCODES` (meta, var, env, kbd, lipsum, placeholder, version, brand). The rule holds in HTML, LaTeX, Typst, and Word, and such a paragraph right after a figure leaves the figure normal with no warning (next-block rule). `{{< embed >}}` and `{{< include >}}` are expanded before the filter and needed no change. A block-valued `meta` still joins (README hint: put a `wrap-clear` div before it). Tests: fixture `tests/stop-shortcode.qmd` with six cases (video, a document `shortcodes:` Lua shortcode `stopbox`, kbd, include, a video right after a figure, a block-valued meta), snapshots in four formats, and the LAYOUT case "video". README "Where wrapping stops" and DESIGN.md (stop rule, next-block rule, step 1) state the rule. Browser check with `tests/layout.js` at 1280x900: each group's next block starts below its figure. 102 renders on macOS and in CI.

**Decisions:** none milestone-local. Promoted D-011 (lone-shortcode stop rule, annotates D-003 and D-007). Plan choices: stop at every shortcode not known to join, over a list of known block shortcodes, and stop at a video in every format, both at the user's choice.
