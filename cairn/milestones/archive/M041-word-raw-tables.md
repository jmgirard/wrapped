# M041: Wrap gt and flextable tables in Word

**Status:** done (2026-10-31, PR #43 https://github.com/jmgirard/wrapped/pull/43)

**Goal:** A `#tbl-` table float whose table is raw Word XML, as gt and flextable write it, wraps the paragraphs after it in Word, with the table set to the full width of its box.

**Outcome:** `wrapped.lua` drops `raw_docx_table` and its raw-XML warning, so a raw openxml table float wraps in Word. In `wrapped-docx.lua`, `floating_table`'s walk calls `fit_raw` on each raw openxml block: the block's first `w:tbl` gets a `w:tblW` of type `dxa` equal to the box width, placed after the `BEFORE_TBLW` elements (`element_end`), in a `w:tblPr` added where there is none (after range markup, before the grid) or opened from a self-closing one; a `w:tblGrid` before the first row scales to the box by rounded edges, and the table's own twip `w:tcW` (typed `dxa` or untyped) scale by the same ratio. gt tables and autofit flextables have no grid, so Word sizes their columns at the box width. `tests/docx-tables.qmd` adds `tbl-gt`, `tbl-flex`, and `tbl-flex-auto` (XML copied from gt 1.4.0 and flextable 0.10.1), rewrites `tbl-raw`, and adds `tbl-raw-empty-pr`, `tbl-raw-bidi`, and `tbl-raw-spaced`; `docx_tables` records `grid=`, `tcw=`, and `tblW-before=` for twip-width inner tables. `tests/docx-raw-tables.qmd` renders the real packages by hand. README, the site pages, DESIGN.md, and the E5 row say these tables wrap; the Word page and the DESIGN.md Known issue add that Word breaks a wide raw table's cell text inside words and that a tall box breaks across pages in Word too.

**Decisions:** D-025 (raw Word XML table floats wrap in Word; supersedes D-023). Milestone-local: none.
